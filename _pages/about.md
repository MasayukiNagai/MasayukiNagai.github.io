## About Me

I'm a third year Ph.D. candidate in Computational Biology at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory [Koo Lab](https://koolab.cshl.edu/). My research focuses on exploring the inner workings of deep learning models to uncover the biological knowledge these models have extracted and identify uncertainty of the models. My interests span mechanistic interpretability, active learning, and model evaluation. Additionally, I am committed to ensuring the safety of artificial intelligence and biotechnology.


## Interests

- **Continual Learning**: refine models' causal understanding by incorporating perturbation data
- **Active Learning**: come up with the most informative experiments
- **Sequence design**: design sequences with desired properties
- **Biosecurity**: ensure prevention of the downside of the dual-use
- **Mechanistic interpretability**: understand inner-workings of the models
